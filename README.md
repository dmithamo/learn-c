# learn-c

This repo tracks my journey through a bunch of books that I am using to learn C.
Yes, I figured it's about time - mosly because:

- I am readin CS:APP, and the implementation languge for exercises there is C. And I want to do al the exercises
- I am learning Rust. Rust, as I understand it, overcomes the shortcomings of C; but waht are the shortcomings of C? I have to know. I just have to.

## Why a repo? 

I read somewhere that you should learn in public - is ot so you have some sort of society pressure? I don't know. Whatever elese it does,
a commit log helps me see the journey I have walked at any point it time. I think of it as a hiker looking back at the terrain they have mastered,
once they get to the peak. It fuels them for the rest of the hike. I have hiked. I know this feeling well. Trust me bro.

## What books am I using?

- K&R C, 2nd ed.
- Programming C: A Modern Approach
- Beej's Guide to C.

The plan is to work through the first one in full, doing all the exercises, or most of them, and refer to the other two as necessary,
to clarify things and stuff.

## CS: APP?

This book is a work of beauty. It endeavours to describe to a programmer what goes on inside a computer, such as allows the code 
they write to actually do something. I am reading this book, and took a detour to learn C, because I want to do all, or most, of the exercises
as the book's author recommends.

## Rust

Yes, I am learning Rust. I felt that adetour through C was needed, to help me see the shortcomings of C that Rust patches over.
