# learn-c

This repo tracks my journey through a bunch of books that I am using to learn C.
Yes, I figured it's about time - mostly because:

- I am reading CS:APP, and the implementation language for exercises there is C. And I want to do all the exercises
- I am learning Rust. Rust, as I understand it, overcomes the shortcomings of C; but what are the shortcomings of C? I have to know. I just have to.

## Why a repo? 

I read somewhere that you should learn in public - is it so you have some sort of societal pressure? I don't know. Whatever else commiting my leaner does,
a commit log helps me see the journey I have walked, at any point in time. I think of it as a hiker looking back at the terrain they have mastered,
once they get to some restpoint. It fuels them for the rest of the hike. I have hiked. I know this feeling well. Trust me bro.

## What books am I using?

- K&R C, 2nd ed.
- Programming C: A Modern Approach
- Beej's Guide to C.

The plan is to work through the first one in full, doing all the exercises, or most of them, and refer to the other two as necessary,
to clarify things and stuff.

## CS: APP

This book is a work of beauty. It endeavours to describe to a programmer what goes on inside a computer, such as allows the code 
they write to actually do something. I am reading this book, and took a detour to learn C, because I want to do all, or most, of the exercises,
as I imagine the book's author intended, if one is to get the most out of the book.

## Rust

Yes, I am learning Rust, the recommended tool for modern systems programming, a domain I have my eyes firmly set on. I felt that a detour through C was needed, at a minimum to help me see the shortcomings of C that Rust patches over.

## SHMG

I have no doubt this journey will help me get a little closer to some mastery of the craft of making software, or at least begin to overcome imposter syndrome, self-taught dev that I am.
SHMG.
